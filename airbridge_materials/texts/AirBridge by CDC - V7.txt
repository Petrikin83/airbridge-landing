Safety
Safety is not a project phase, it is a continuous discipline.
CDC's HSE management framework is active from the first site visit to final handover, with risk controls adapted to the specific hazards of each ropeway project.​
Proactive risk management​
Formal risk assessments are conducted before each phase of the project. Hazards are identified, evaluated, and mitigated beforehand, not after an incident.
Safe execution planning
​
Method statements, toolbox talks, and site safety plans are developed in coordination with all contractors. CDC ensures that safe working practices are embedded in every team on site.​
CDC embeds quality management throughout every project phase, not as a final check, but as a controlled execution standard.
Our procedures are designed to catch non-conformities early, when they are inexpensive to resolve.​
