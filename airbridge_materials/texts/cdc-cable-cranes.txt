Cable Cranes
Overview
CDC is an experienced developer and operator of cable crane systems, with extensive expertise in the design, installation, and operation of rope-based lifting and transport solutions in challenging environments.
Cable cranes enable the efficient movement of materials, equipment, infrastructure components, and personnel across steep slopes, valleys, and hard-to-access areas, where conventional machinery cannot operate safely or efficiently.

complex construction sites
How
it works
A cable crane is a lifting device in which the load-bearing tracks for the trolley are steel cables stretched between towers. It is designed for transporting loads vertically and horizontally over long distances.
The movement of the carriage to the top – occurs due to the traction rope being pulled onto a winch located at the top of the slope.
The carriage designed for lifting and transporting cargo. It uses force of winch to moving uphill and gravity for moving downhill

AirBridge®
cable cranes:
Advanced cable crane solution for flexible,
long-distance operations
AirBridge® cable cranes represent the evolution of traditional rope crane systems, maintaining the same core capabilities while significantly expanding operational flexibility.
By eliminating the need for a traction rope and fixed winch, the system enables longer routes, greater independence from ground infrastructure, and more adaptable configurations across complex terrain.
