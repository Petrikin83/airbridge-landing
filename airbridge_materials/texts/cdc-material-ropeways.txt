Material Ropeways
Overview
CDC material ropeways provide continuous transport of bulk materials across mountains, valleys, rivers, and remote extraction sites, where conventional road infrastructure is costly, slow to build, or environmentally disruptive.
Designed for industrial logistics, they reduce truck traffic, minimize ground impact, and support efficient long-distance material movement in steep or hard-to-access environments.
Replace haul roads
in difficult terrain
How
it works
A material ropeway includes terminal and sometimes intermediate stations.
Between them, two track ropes carry loaded and empty cars.
The stations are equipped with drive and tensioning systems, along with tracks, conveyors, and loading/unloading devices.
Along the route, supports hold the track ropes and guide the haulage rope, which forms a continuous loop around pulleys at the stations.
The drive and tensioning systems are usually located at opposite ends.
Key
Advantages
Continuous bulk material transport over long distances
Reduced need for haul roads and truck fleets
Minimal environmental and ground impact
Efficient operation in steep or remote terrain
Lower operating costs compared to road logistics
