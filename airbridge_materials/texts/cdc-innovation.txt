Overview
Innovation at CDC Cableway Development Company is driven by real infrastructure challenges. Through applied research, patented technologies, and operational deployment, we develop ropeway systems designed to overcome the technical and economic limits of conventional ropeway transportation systems.
CDC’s approach to innovation is practical and engineering-led: technologies are developed to be built, certified, and operated in real-world conditions, delivering measurable improvements in efficiency, feasibility, and lifecycle performance.
AirBridge®
Patented Self-Propelled Ropeway Technology
AirBridge® is CDC’s proprietary self-propelled ropeway system, redefining how aerial mobility infrastructure is designed and deployed in complex environments.
Developed for urban, mountainous, and remote terrains where conventional roads, bridges, rail systems, or traditional ropeways are inefficient or impractical, AirBridge® provides a flexible alternative for passenger and material transport.
AirBridge® is a fully developed and operational system, engineered for real-world deployment, which in some environments can substitute traditional ropeway systems, bridges, and cranes.
What is
AirBridge®
ONE MODULAR SYSTEM. MULTIPLE APPLICATIONS.
AirBridge® is a modular ropeway system based on a self-propelled unit that travels along a static rope.
Each module integrates its own power and traction system, enabling independent movement without centralized drive stations.

Why AirBridge®
is Different
AirBridge® is designed to reduce infrastructure complexity and improve operational efficiency by:
Limiting the number of pylons and ground supports
Optimizing energy use through modular, self-propelled units
Allowing system capacity to be deployed according to real operational demand
Traditional ropeways typically operate below 10% average utilization, whereas AirBridge® can achieve up to 100% efficiency by activating capacity on demand.

Enhanced financial viability and faster payback for projects.
Certification and Regulatory
Compliance
AirBridge® is engineered for regulated infrastructure environments and designed in compliance with EU Regulation 2016/424 on cableway installations, along with applicable international safety and quality standards.
Regulatory compliance is embedded from the earliest design stages, ensuring operational safety, structural reliability, and long-term performance across diverse markets.

Use
Cases
AirBridge® supports modular ropeway applications for passenger, cargo, and material transport across urban, mountainous, remote, and extreme environments.
Passenger
Ropeways
AirBridge® enables efficient passenger transport across urban, mountainous, and remote environments. The system supports personnel mobility, tourism access, and urban connectivity while reducing reliance on ground infrastructure and minimizing environmental impact.
Urban Transportation
AirBridge-powered ropeway systems offer an eco-efficient mobility solution for urban and peri-urban areas. By reducing reliance on ground infrastructure, they help alleviate traffic congestion while minimizing land use and environmental impact.
Mountain Resorts
In high-altitude contexts, ropeway systems ensure smooth and comfortable year-round transport for visitors and staff. AirBridge technology enables adaptable configurations suited for demanding climatic and topographical conditions.
Tourism & Area Development
AirBridge supports ropeway systems that provide sustainable and efficient access to natural and cultural attractions. By minimizing ground impact while enabling panoramic mobility, these solutions enhance visitor experience without compromising environmental integrity.
Personnel Transportation
AirBridge enables ropeway systems designed for safe and efficient personnel transport to remote construction sites, industrial facilities, and challenging locations where traditional roads are impractical.
