Passenger Ropeways
Overview
CDC passenger ropeways provide efficient, low-impact mobility across urban, mountain, and remote environments, supporting personnel transport, tourism access, resort operations, and everyday connectivity.
Designed for safe and reliable movement in complex terrain, they reduce reliance on ground infrastructure, limit land use, ease congestion, and minimize environmental impact while improving access to destinations that are difficult to reach by road.
Everyday transport for
challenging environments
How
it works
Passenger ropeways provide efficient, continuous transport across terrain where conventional infrastructure is impractical or impossible. CDC delivers turnkey systems for tourism, urban mobility, and personnel transport, minimizing environmental impact and ground construction.
Key application
scenarios
Personnel transportation
Safe and efficient personnel transport to remote construction sites, industrial facilities, and challenging locations where traditional roads are impractical.
Urban transportation
Eco-efficient mobility solution for urban and peri-urban areas.
By reducing reliance on ground infrastructure, our ropeways help alleviate traffic congestion while minimizing land use and environmental impact.
Tourism development
Sustainable and efficient access to natural and cultural attractions. By minimizing ground impact while enabling panoramic mobility, our solutions enhance visitor experience without compromising environmental integrity.
Mountain resorts
In high-altitude contexts, ropeway systems ensure smooth and comfortable year-round transport for visitors and staff.
AirBridge®
passenger ropeways
Boarding and unloading at any point
AirBridge® enables cabins or carriers to stop and be lowered at any point along the line, allowing passengers, cargo, or materials to be loaded and unloaded exactly where needed. This creates a highly flexible system that operates independently from fixed stations, improving accessibility and adaptability across complex routes.
AirBridge® application
scenarios
Personnel transportation
We are setting a new standard for industrial mobility by offering a more efficient and resilient alternative to ground transportation for uninterrupted access to remote sites in the harshest environments.
Urban transportation
By surpassing the capabilities of ground transportation, our intelligent cable mobility systems radically reduce congestion in the urban network, offering a faster and more environmentally friendly alternative at a fraction of the cost of infrastructure and land acquisition.
Tourism development
Rope systems open up entirely new horizons for adventure, turning travel into an adventure with limitless possibilities for tourists.
Mountain resorts
AirBridge® technology enables adaptable configurations suited for demanding climatic and topographical conditions.
