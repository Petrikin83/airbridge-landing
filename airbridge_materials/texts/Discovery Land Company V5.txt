The B2B Value
•
Zero-Emission Operations: generates clean electricity on demand using green ammonia,
producing absolutely zero CO2 emissions - ideal for ecologically sensitive areas, mountain
resorts, and green urban mobility.
•
Maximum Off-Grid Independence: ammonia offers superior volumetric energy density
compared to hydrogen and batteries. This ensures extended, uninterrupted operation without
the massive CAPEX required to build heavy electrical grids across complex terrain.
•
Safe & Scalable Logistics: unlike raw hydrogen, ammonia is easily transported and stored at
ambient conditions, leveraging existing global infrastructure for safe and cost-effective refueling
in remote environments.
