===== SLIDE 2 =====
What is
AirBridge®
is a modular ropeway system based on a self-propelled unit that travels along a static rope.
Each module integrates its own power and traction system, enabling independent movement without centralized drive stations.

===== SLIDE 3 =====
The
technology
The
AirBridge®
module can be powered by diesel, electric, or
hybrid
systems, and may also function as a backup power solution, enhancing operational resilience.
AirBridge®
is protected by international patent (WO2017064014) and incorporates 52 technical innovations.
Thanks to its advanced engineering architecture,
AirBridge®
operates efficiently in environments where traditional ropeways, cranes, ground-based infrastructure would demand significant civil works or prove technically impractical.

Certification

AirBridge®
is engineered for regulated infrastructure environments and designed in compliance with EU Regulation 2016/424 on cableway installations, along with applicable international safety and quality standards.
Regulatory compliance is embedded from the earliest design stages, ensuring operational safety, structural reliability, and long-term performance across diverse markets.

===== SLIDE 7 =====
Why
AirBridge
®
is different
AirBridge®
is designed to reduce infrastructure complexity and improve operational efficiency by:
Limiting the number of pylons and ground supports
Optimizing energy use through modular, self-propelled units
Allowing system capacity to be deployed according to real operational demand
Traditional ropeways typically operate below 10% average utilization, whereas
AirBridge®
can achieve up to 100% efficiency by activating capacity on demand.
Lower CAPEX
Minimized structural and civil engineering costs
Lower OPEX
Efficient performance with reduced lifecycle expenses
Accelerated ROI
Enhanced financial viability and faster payback for projects.
