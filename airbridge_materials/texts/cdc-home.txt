CDC Cableway
Development Company
We are a leading turn-key ropeway provider and the developer of AirBridge® patented ropeway technology. As one of the key members of the Ropeway Project Group, we can deliver end-to-end ropeway solutions: from feasibility studies and engineering to high-altitude installation, commissioning, and long-term operation and maintenance.
Working together with other ropeway professionals from the Ropeway Project Group, and key ropeway providers globally, we successfully deliver turn-key, reliable, scalable, and future-ready ropeway infrastructure for the world’s most demanding terrains.

CDC Lab
: ropeway research & engineering innovation
CDC Lab is our dedicated research and development site, driving continuous innovation in ropeway engineering and high-performance transport systems.
Through CDC Lab, we develop next-generation ropeway technologies, optimize structural and mechanical performance, and transform research into certified, scalable, and operational systems. Our R&D activities are directly connected to small scale projects, ensuring every innovation is tested, validated, and deployed under real-world operational conditions.
