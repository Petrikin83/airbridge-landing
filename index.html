The Innovation

AirBridge® — Patented Self-Propelled Ropeway Technology

AirBridge® is CDC's proprietary self-propelled ropeway system, redefining how aerial mobility infrastructure is designed and deployed in complex environments.

Developed for urban, mountainous, and remote terrains where conventional roads, bridges, rail systems, or traditional ropeways are inefficient or impractical, AirBridge® provides a flexible alternative for passenger and material transport.

Engineered for regulated environments and compliant with EU Regulation 2016/424, the system embeds safety and quality at every stage. Its zero-footprint approach uses aerial installation via heavy helicopters — no temporary roads — dramatically accelerating deployment in sensitive terrain.

Solutions

One modular system. Multiple applications.

AirBridge® is a modular ropeway system based on a self-propelled unit that travels along a static rope — delivering flexible passenger and material transport across three core solutions.

01

Passenger Ropeways

Efficient, low-impact mobility across urban, mountain, and remote environments — supporting personnel transport, tourism access, resort operations, and everyday connectivity.

  • Boarding & unloading at any point along the line
  • Urban, tourism, resort & personnel transport
  • Panoramic, silent mobility
  • Year-round operation in demanding terrain
02

Material Ropeways

Continuous transport of bulk materials across mountains, valleys, rivers, and remote extraction sites where road infrastructure is costly, slow to build, or environmentally disruptive.

  • Continuous bulk transport over long distances
  • Reduced haul roads & truck fleets
  • Minimal environmental and ground impact
  • Lower operating costs vs. road logistics
03

Cable Cranes

Experienced development and operation of cable crane systems for lifting and transport across steep slopes and hard-to-access areas where conventional machinery cannot operate safely.

  • No roads or heavy ground machinery required
  • Materials, equipment & personnel in one system
  • Fast installation in the most difficult terrain
  • AirBridge®: long-distance, self-propelled operation
